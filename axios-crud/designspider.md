# Design Breakdown — Movie Carousel & Concept Board Style

This document summarizes the visual design patterns used in the two reference mockups: a **hero carousel slide** (movie streaming/browse style) and a **concept/pitch board** (behind-the-scenes design breakdown).

---

## 1. Hero Carousel Slide

### Layout
- Full-bleed dark background (near-black, deep navy) with a large character portrait as the focal point, positioned right-of-center.
- Left-aligned text block: studio wordmark → title (large display type) → tagline/subtitle → character name → actor role label.
- Thin horizontal accent lines (red) bracket the content area top and bottom, giving the panel a "framed slide" feel.
- Bottom-left corner: content rating badge + production company logos in a single row.
- Bottom-left, below logos: cast line ("Stars: ...") with a "See full cast & crew »" link in muted text.
- Right side: slide index number (e.g. "1" with superscript total count), a short descriptive blurb (2–3 lines, right-aligned), and a circular red play button below it.
- Subtle ambient particles/embers overlaid on the image for atmosphere.

### Typography
- Studio wordmark: small, bold, condensed sans, white.
- Main title: large, heavy weight (bold/black), slight gold-to-white gradient or foil effect, tracked tightly.
- Subtitle: thin/light weight, wide letter-spacing, muted gold or white.
- Body copy (blurb, cast line): small, clean sans-serif, light weight, high line-height for readability over the dark background.

### Color Palette
- Background: `#05070C` – `#0B0F17` (near-black navy)
- Accent line/CTA: `#D8232A` (red)
- Title highlight: `#F5D48A` / `#FFFFFF` gradient (gold-white)
- Body text: `#C9CDD6` (soft gray-white) at reduced opacity

### Key UI Elements
- Slide index counter (current/total)
- Circular play button (primary CTA)
- Rating + studio badge row
- "See full cast & crew" link affordance

## 2. Concept / Pitch Board

### Layout
- Blueprint-style background (grid pattern, engineering-drawing aesthetic) in blue.
- Physical "desk" props scattered at the edges (camera, glasses, photo prints, newspaper clipping) to simulate a designer's real workspace — a common technique in portfolio/pitch presentations.
- Centered logo lockup with a secondary subtitle beneath it.
- Two small reference photos stacked vertically just under the title, showing costume front/back reference.
- Repeated icon motif (three variations of a spider emblem) placed to the right of the reference photos, showing iteration/exploration.
- Hand-written style annotations and sketch marks scattered around the board (e.g. callouts like "too dramatic," "too clean," directional notes), simulating an artist's marginalia/critique notes.
- Footer: studio/agency signature logo + small-print rights/ownership disclaimer, centered at the very bottom.

### Typography
- Logo lockup: bold, italicized comic-style display font with a colored outline/drop-shadow — evokes a hand-lettered comic logotype.
- Subtitle: condensed, all-caps, wide tracking, contrasting color from the main logo.
- Annotation text: casual handwriting-style font, small, low-contrast, scattered at angles to mimic real margin notes.
- Corner labels ("Pics Used," "Font, Title Textures," etc.): small caps, thin weight, high letter-spacing, used as a legend/credits key.

### Color Palette
- Background grid: `#1B4F91` – `#2A6BC2` (blueprint blue) with lighter grid lines `#4E86C7`
- Logo primary: `#D8232A` (red) with `#F4D976` (gold) outline
- Prop/photo elements: desaturated neutrals to contrast against the blue field
- Annotation ink: `#EAF2FB` (near-white, low opacity)

### Key Composition Techniques
- **Moodboard framing**: physical objects at the corners create a photographed "flat lay" illusion.
- **Iteration display**: showing 2–3 variants of the same icon signals a design-exploration narrative.
- **Annotation layer**: sketchy notes placed over a clean grid contrast "process" against "polish."
- **Credit/legend corners**: small utility text in the four corners documents sources and rights, framing the whole board as a professional case-study slide.

## Reusable Takeaways
| Element | Carousel Slide | Concept Board |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Solid dark, atmospheric | Blueprint grid, technical |
| Focal point | Large character render | Central logo lockup |
| Supporting text | Marketing copy (blurb, cast) | Process notes (annotations) |
| Accent color | Red (CTA-driven) | Red + gold (brand-driven) |
| Framing device | Thin horizontal rules | Scattered desk props |
